Body Weight Daily Dose

Note: 1 tsp = 5 mL. Do not exceed recommended daily dose. Combine dietary supplements with a balanced diet and healthy lifestyle for best results.

Warning

Consult your physician before using BalanceOil™ if you are:

  • Pregnant
  • Nursing
  • Taking prescription drugs
  • Have a medical condition

Important: Do not give BalanceOil™ to children under four years of age.

Storage

  • Unopened bottles: Store in a dark dry place at room temperature or in the refrigerator.
  • Opened bottles: Store in refrigerator and use within 45 days.
  • Note: The oil may become cloudy at temperatures lower than 4°C (39°F) due to the solidification of the olive oil. The oil will clear at room temperature.
  • Safety: Keep out of reach of children.
