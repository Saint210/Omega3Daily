Frequently Asked Questions

Earning ZinoCash

When do I start earning ZinoCash?

You start earning ZinoCash from your very first order. Level 1 benefits (shipping rewards) are available immediately.

How do I reach Level 2?

After completing 7 orders, you automatically unlock Level 2 and start earning 10% back in ZinoCash on all purchases.

Do subscription orders count?

Yes, all orders count towards your Level 2 qualification, including both one-time purchases and subscription orders.

Redeeming ZinoCash

How do I use my ZinoCash?

Simply add products to your cart and select "ZinoCash" as your payment method during checkout.

Can I use ZinoCash for subscriptions?

No, ZinoCash can only be used for single-item purchases, not for subscription orders.

Is there a minimum redemption amount?

No, you can redeem any amount of ZinoCash as long as you have enough to cover your purchase.

Account Management

Does my ZinoCash expire?

No, your ZinoCash never expires as long as you remain an active customer with at least one purchase every 12 months.

Can I transfer my ZinoCash?

Yes, you can transfer your ZinoCash to family members who are also Omega3Daily customers.

How do I check my ZinoCash balance?

Log into your account dashboard to view your current ZinoCash balance and earning history.
