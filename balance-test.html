Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Omega-3 Index test?

The Omega-3 Index measures the amount of EPA and DHA omega-3 fatty acids in your red blood cell membranes, giving you a long-term view of your omega-3 status. A higher level typically indicates better long-term omega-3 status.

How do I check my Omega-3 levels?

With the Zinzino Balance test, you can easily test your omega-3 levels at home, without any need for a doctor's visit. Our testing kit contains everything you need to complete the finger prick test.

Is an Omega-3 test worth it?

Understanding your omega-3 levels can be highly valuable. It allows you to make informed decisions about your diet and supplementation and gives you a benchmark to compare against going forward.

How much does the Omega-3 Index cost?

The Zinzino Balance test is competitively priced at $170.00 USD (Retail Price) or $119.00 USD (Premier Price). A worthy investment in understanding your health.

At-Home vs. Doctor's Blood Draw

Our dried blood spot technology is highly accurate and comparable to a blood draw. It is less invasive and can be completed in the comfort of your own home.

Omega 6:3 Ratio vs. Omega-3 Index

The Omega 6:3 ratio refers to the balance between Omega 6 and Omega 3 fatty acids. The Omega-3 index measures EPA and DHA in red blood cell membranes.
